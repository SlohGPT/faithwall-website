## What was decided, and why

There were three options:
- **Merge as built.** This would publish a new URL on the first day of a hold Karol's refresh had just declared, on risk grounds.
- **Leave it unmerged.** This would block the cloud routine for a week, including p53, the top queue item and the one fix the refresh wants shipped now.
- **Quarantine it.** Quarantine is reserved for work that fails verification, and this work doesn't.

The choice was a fourth option: **merge it and hold it unpublished.** The verified work is kept in main and the gate is cleared, so the pipeline doesn't stall. Nothing goes live against the hold. The hold is one reversible commit, and Karol decides at the 10-26 refresh.

This follows the merge gate's rule for queue collisions (3.e2): finished work is not discarded over a decision made without knowledge of the build. It differs in one way. A demotion is a priority change, but this hold is a risk control, so the post waits instead of going live. `isPublished: false` is a first-class switch in this codebase. `src/lib/posts.ts` filters listings, related posts and pillar featured lists on it, and `scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` and the prerender skip it.
