## What was decided, and why

Rejected and quarantined. The gate's outreach check says drafts must contain no fabricated claims presented as fact. A false statement about cost to a reviewer, in Karol's name, is that, and the wording is banned outright. So this was a clear fail, not an ambiguous call.

The line wasn't edited on the way in, because a gate that rewrites content would be landing text nobody verified, and the brief says failing content never reaches main. Quarantine costs little here: p28 has 14 days until its 11-16 send-by, and a rerun from main picks up the two refresh notes this branch couldn't have seen.
