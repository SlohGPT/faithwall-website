# Pricing — FaithWall

FaithWall is an iPhone app that puts the Bible verses you choose on your lock screen.
It is free to download, and using it requires a FaithWall subscription. There is no free tier.

## Price

The price, billing period and any trial offer are shown in the App Store before you pay, in your local currency, and may change over time.

## What you get

- Pick your own verse by book, chapter and verse or by search, in 24 translations
- Show it as a full-screen lock-screen wallpaper (applied by an Apple Shortcut) or as a compact lock-screen widget that shows the verse you picked (an optional "Auto-rotate verses" setting (off by default) makes it show random verses instead, changing every hour, every 8 hours or once a day)
- App interface in 10 languages
- Built-in Bible browser (all books and chapters) for finding your verse; built for the lock screen, not a study app

## Platforms

- iOS 15.0 or later (the lock-screen widget needs iOS 16)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lock-screen-bible-verse/id6756815070

## Payments

Subscriptions are billed through Apple and can be managed or cancelled in your App Store account settings.
